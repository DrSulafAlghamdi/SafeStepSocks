# Risk register

| Risk | Priority | Mitigation and acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall is missed (false negative) | Critical | Independent sensitivity tests across ages/activities; communicate that alerts are not guaranteed; no safety reliance |
| False alarm, alarm fatigue | High | Test negative activities and report false alerts by exposure time and by trial |
| Battery, wireless, or phone outage | High | Battery monitoring, delivery acknowledgment, offline behavior and fault alerts (future work) |
| Compression pressure or skin irritation | High | Pressure testing, sizing, materials assessment, appropriate contraindication screening |
| Electronic heat, moisture, breakage or ingestion hazard | High | Removeable enclosed electronics, lab electrical safety and ingress assessment |
| Slip-resistant soles do not reliably reduce slipping | High | Wet/dry standardized traction testing across surfaces and aging |
| Privacy/data leakage | High | Minimal data collection, encryption, permission controls and retention policy |
| Clinical and regulatory misclassification | High | Obtain guidance on Saudi SFDA classification and applicable approvals |
| Supply-chain/cost volatility | Medium | Alternative parts, BOM revisions and manufacturing quality agreements |
| Intellectual property disclosure | High | Private repository and patent/IP review before public demo or publishing details |

Every proposed mitigation must eventually have a test, owner and acceptance criterion; this table is a planning document, not evidence that risk has been eliminated.
