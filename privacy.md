# Privacy and human-subject data

- Collect the **minimum** motion and event information needed for research, and use pseudonymous trial IDs.
- Do not put participant names, exact addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers, diagnoses, medical records or unconsented recordings into this repository.
- Store identifiable consent records separately in access-restricted systems. Keep raw trial records private and apply a documented retention schedule.
- Define consent, lawful basis, access controls and security measures with the responsible institution. Review Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) obligations and any clinical research requirements.
- Future alert APIs must authenticate devices/caregivers, encrypt data in transit and at rest, log access, handle missed delivery and permit data removal as appropriate.
- Sample data is synthetically generated; do not replace it with identifiable recordings.
