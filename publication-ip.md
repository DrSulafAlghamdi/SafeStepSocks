# Intellectual property and public disclosure

**Recommended default: keep this repository private.** Early public disclosures of technical inventions can affect patent novelty and commercialization options depending on jurisdiction and timing. Obtain advice from a qualified patent/IP professional or your institutional innovation office **before** publishing proprietary algorithms, mechanical designs, detailed photos, testing results or manufacturing specifications.

This repository is delivered **without a license**. A public GitHub repository without a license does **not** automatically grant others rights to reproduce, distribute or modify it. Choose appropriate licensing intentionally after ownership and IP review.

Never share names or affiliations in ways that imply an individual or healthcare organization approved, endorsed or partnered with the project without their authorization. Never upload identifiable health data.
