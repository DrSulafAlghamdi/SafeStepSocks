# Market need and competitor landscape — research outline

SafeStepSocks is positioned at the intersection of anti-slip footwear, wearable motion sensing, and caregiver alerting. This document is a *research outline*, not an up-to-date market valuation or verified purchasing analysis.

## Existing alternatives to benchmark

| Type | Example | Differentiation to investigate | Important limitation of SafeStepSocks claims |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hospital sensor socks | Palarum PUP smart socks | Could target more independent, portable use | Other smart socks already combine sensing and protective tread; never claim to be first |
| Gait-monitoring socks | Sensoria | Could include explicit post-event alerting | Compare actual specs and clinical performance before claiming superiority |
| Foot-temperature socks | Siren | Different primary indication | These devices address other clinical needs, not direct fall detection |
| Smartwatches | Apple Watch hard-fall detection | Physical grip plus sensing in a sock | Robustness of foot-worn detection is not established |
| Non-slip patient socks | Hospital grip socks | Electronic monitoring plus traction | Anti-slip products may be cheaper and do not require charging |
| Room-mounted sensors | Radar or camera-based room systems | Portability outside monitored room | Room systems avoid charging or wearing a device |

## Evidence to gather before public pitch claims

1. **Epidemiology:** international and Saudi age-related fall burden from official authorities and peer-reviewed clinical literature.
2. **Procurement:** official tenders specifying slip-resistant patient socks, fall-sensing devices or monitoring systems, including tender ID and date.
3. **Independent clinical performance:** peer-reviewed smart-sock evaluations, with study design and sample size.
4. **Market size:** source, currency, baseline year, forecast year, product-category definition and report date.
5. **Customer demand:** interviews, non-binding letters of interest, pilot inquiries and actual purchase commitments, clearly labeled.

Do not present a market research category valuation as SafeStepSocks' own achievable revenue. Quantify SAM/SOM only after defining prices, buying centers, regulations, and addressable customer segments.
