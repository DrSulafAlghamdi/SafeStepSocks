# Evidence register — what we can and cannot currently claim

This register helps prepare accurate grant, pitch and hackathon materials. **Reported** does not mean independently verified.

| Claim | Current basis | Public-facing treatment | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SafeStepSocks combines anti-slip materials, motion sensors, alerts, compression and warmth | Product design concept | Intended / proposed features | BOM, pictures and prototype verification |
| Preliminary testing with 50 adults over age 40 | Project-team report | Label as *team-reported preliminary testing*, pending records | Protocol, consent, anonymized participant count and results |
| False alarm rate reduced to 2% after development | Project-team report | Do not state as independently confirmed | Before/after logs, denominator, algorithm versions and held-out trial results |
| Faster response or fewer falls | Not established | Do not claim established health outcomes | Controlled comparison and properly designed clinical studies |
| Professional healthcare interest | Team-reported discussions | Do not describe as endorsement or partnership without permission | Dated written confirmation and approved quote |
| Interest from prospective hospital adopters | Potential target organizations identified | Describe as *prospective partners* only | Letter of interest, pilot agreement or purchase order |
| Environmental benefit | Modular replacement is proposed | Potential benefit, not quantified footprint reduction | Lifecycle analysis and reuse testing |

This repository deliberately contains **no fabricated pilot results**, endorsements, quotations or clinical datasets.
